# Product Requirements Document (PRD) — Tiar Property Pitching Tools Portal

## 1. Executive Summary & Goals
- **Product Name:** Tiar Property Client Pitching & Interactive Closing Tools Portal
- **Primary User:** Mas Tiar, Inbound Sales Closer, & Property Advisors Tiar Property.
- **Target Audience:** First-time homebuyers, young families, millennial couples (Surabaya & Sidoarjo), looking for homes in Kwangsan & The Oso Waru/MERR.
- **Core Problem:** Explaining KPR interest rates, developer subsidies, joint income limits, and price comparisons verbally often creates confusion and price shock (resistance to Rp 400 Jt - Rp 650 Jt+ prices).
- **Solution:** A tactile, clean, light-mode interactive presentation suite that can be operated seamlessly on an iPad/tablet or laptop during surveys, cafe meetings, or Zoom calls.

## 2. Scope & Core Tool Specifications

### Tool 1: Interactive KPR Accumulator & Subsidy Engine
- Sliders: Property Price (Preset: Rp 435 Jt Kwangsan, Rp 650 Jt The Oso, Custom slider Rp 300 Jt - Rp 1.5 M).
- DP Mode: DP 0% (Developer Subsidy) vs Custom DP (5%, 10%, 20%).
- Tenor: 5, 10, 15, 20, 25, 30 Tahun.
- Fixed Interest Promo: 3.4% s/d 4.85% (Bulan 1-12/24/36).
- Floating Interest: 8.5% s/d 10.5%.
- **Tiar Developer Advantage:**
  - Toggle: Subsidi Angsuran Rp 1.400.000 / bln (6 s/d 12 Bulan).
  - Toggle: Free All-In (BPHTB, AJB, BBN, Biaya Notaris — hemat Rp 25-35 Jt).
  - Toggle: Subsidi Biaya KPR s/d Rp 20 Jt.
- **Psychological Output:**
  - Real Net Monthly Installment during promo.
  - Micro-Installment Daily Equivalence: "Hanya Rp 60.000 / hari (setara 2 gelas kopi)".
  - Total Savings Display: Total bonus & subsidi developer dinikmati konsumen.

### Tool 2: Interactive Scratchpad & Whiteboard Canvas
- Responsive canvas optimized for stylus, touch, and mouse.
- Tools: Pencil/Pen, Highlighter, Eraser, Undo, Redo, Clear Canvas, Stroke Thickness.
- Colors: Carbon Ink, Bank Emerald Green, Alert Amber, Blueprint Blue.
- Templates: Blank Paper, Dot Grid, House Layout Foundation (1-Lt & 2-Lt), Block Siteplan.
- Instant Export: Download PNG image or copy to clipboard for WhatsApp sharing.

### Tool 3: Joint Income & Debt Burden Ratio (DBR 40%) Analyzer
- Inputs: Gaji Suami, Gaji Istri, Cicilan Aktif Lain (motor, kartu kredit, pinjol).
- Real-time DBR Meter: Green (<35%), Amber (35-40%), Red (>40%).
- Max Installment Allowed by Bank Rekanan (BTN, Mandiri, BRI, BSI, BNI).
- Diagnostic Advice: "Tambahkan Joint Income istri untuk menaikkan plafon KPR hingga Rp 450 Jt".

### Tool 4: Rent vs Buy Financial Comparison Engine
- Inputs: Sewa kontrakan per tahun (default: Rp 25 Jt - Rp 35 Jt).
- Comparison Metrics across 5, 10, 15 years:
  - Total Rental Expense (100% money burnt, 0 asset value, projected rent increases).
  - Tiar Property Equity (100% ownership, SHM split, rising asset value).
- Interactive Bar Chart showing the financial gap over time.

### Tool 5: Capital Gain & Infrastructure Catalyst Projector
- Inputs: Target hold duration (3, 5, 10 tahun).
- Catalysts: RSUD Sedati (2027 completion), Flyover Aloha operational impact, OERR/MERR corridor connectivity.
- Projected Asset Value & Capital Gain ROI (Conservative 10-12%/year vs Catalyst 30-45% bump).

### Tool 6: Official Slip Generator & 1-Click WhatsApp Closer
- Summarizes the active pitch state into a clean branded digital slip.
- Formats an authentic 3-balloon human WhatsApp chat message with client name and exact figures, opening direct `wa.me` links with 1 click.

## 3. Strict Domain Guardrails
- **Confidentiality:** DILARANG KERAS mencantumkan atau menyebut istilah "BLT" (Bantuan Likuiditas Transaksi / insentif internal marketing).
- **Language & Tone:** Clean Indonesian, clear professional terminology, no jargon confusion.
